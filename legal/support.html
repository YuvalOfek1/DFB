I bought Foldwise but it's still locked

Open Foldwise and tap Restore Purchases on the home screen. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with, and that you have a connection.

How do I use it on a new phone?

Install Foldwise, sign in to the same store account, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase moves with your account. Your lists stay on the old phone, so set up the force words again.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Foldwise. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Foldwise. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. You keep access until the end of the paid month.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund page.

Where are the settings?

Touch and hold the big "Notes" title. That opens the secret Setup screen with your lists, force words, language and the How to Perform guide.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Once unlocked, the trick works offline. It only goes online to buy or restore a purchase.
