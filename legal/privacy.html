Foldwise

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short: Foldwise has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your lists and settings stay on your phone. The only data that leaves it is what's needed to process and confirm your purchase.

Who we are

Foldwise is a magic trick app made by Yuval Ofek ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email yuvalofek97@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Foldwise stores these only on your device, and never sends them to us or anyone else:

  • Your lists, their titles and items, and the force word chosen for each list
  • Your settings, such as the app language
  • Whether your purchase is active, so the app can open without a connection

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and unlock the app, including when you restore purchases on a new phone. To do that, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random anonymous ID that the app creates on your phone. It isn't linked to your name, email or store account details.
  • Your Foldwise purchase history: which plan, its price and currency, the purchase and renewal dates, and the store's transaction IDs.
  • Technical details needed to make the connection, such as the app version, the operating system version, the device model, the store country and your IP address.

We use this only to give you access to what you bought and to see overall sales figures. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier

We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Internet connection

The trick works without a connection. The app goes online only to check, buy or restore your purchase through the store and RevenueCat.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or the app. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat, Apple and Google for as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access or delete the purchase data held about you, or object to how it's used. Email us with the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt so we can find the right record. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Foldwise isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its store listing.
